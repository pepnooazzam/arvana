Built Around Exceptional Materials

The character of every piece begins with the material. We carefully select timber based on natural durability, wood grain, moisture content, and intended application.

Teak (Jati)

Tectona grandis

Naturally durable, stable, and rich in character. Ideal for both premium indoor statement furniture and luxury outdoor resort lounge sets.

Mahogany (Mahoni)

Swietenia macrophylla

A versatile hardwood with a refined, interlocking grain and warm appearance, widely used for premium indoor furniture and continuous-grain credenzas.

White Oak (Ek)

Quercus alba

Strong, distinctive, and contemporary, offering a natural pale appearance suited to modern minimalist and Japandi architectural interior collections.

Reclaimed Wood

Salvaged Antique Teak

Selected antique timber salvaged from historic Javanese homes that brings authentic texture, deep historical patina, and sustainable character.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Do you manufacture custom furniture? +
Yes. We manufacture bespoke furniture based on 2D technical drawings, 3D CAD models, hand sketches, moodboard images, dimensions, or existing product references. Our internal engineering team translates your creative vision into precise production shop drawings.
What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +
For serial production, our standard MOQ is typically 10 to 20 pieces per design, or a consolidated 20ft Full Container Load (FCL) across multiple models. For hospitality projects, we accommodate customized quantities to match your specific room counts.
Can you produce OEM and private label furniture? +
Yes. Over 60% of our export production is private-label manufacturing for international furniture brands, design studios, and retailers. We honor strict non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and apply your custom branding, stamp marks, barcodes, and packaging.
Can you make a prototype before volume production? +
Yes. We strongly encourage prototype sample development for new collections. Prototyping typically takes 3 to 4 weeks, with detailed video inspection or direct courier shipment for physical sign-off.
How long does production take? +
Typical production lead times range from 6 to 10 weeks depending on order volume and timber specification, starting from deposit confirmation and technical drawing sign-off.
